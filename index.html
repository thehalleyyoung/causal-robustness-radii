How many graph edits can your conclusion survive?

Enumerate nearby causal DAGs, recompute their adjustment sets, and find the smallest edit that overturns a specified conclusion on the same data.

The quantity

A minimum over an explicit neighbourhood

Each addition, deletion or reversal counts as one edge edit. Search all allowed acyclic graphs up to the chosen budget. Return the first failure and its witness edits; if none fails, report a bound.

Two questions, two radii

Claim-preserving: how many edits change the adjustment conclusion while retaining a causal path?

Unrestricted: how many edits overturn it, including deleting the causal path?

A finite floating-point certificate is conditional on the data, adjustment rule, estimator and edit permissions.

Fresh matched numerical intervention

Measurement origins exposed an avoidable failure

Twelve new synthetic datasets were tested at ordinary origins and at offsets of ±10¹⁴. Scaling alone falsely rejected every translated full-rank regression. Centering before scaling restored every baseline and radius class.

Independent statsmodels HC3 agreed on the identical translated float inputs. A milder ±10⁸ control passed before repair and is retained.

Dataset is the unit; n=120; severe holdout seeds 8200–8211; search K=1. Large offsets quantize inputs. These are numerical controls, not causal-ground-truth observations.

Scope and unresolved comparisons

Exact search does not establish causal truth

Radii describe a specified fixed-data predicate. They do not provide interval arithmetic, validate a DAG, or justify significance after graph search. Historical synthetic rankings and real-data case studies remain archival evidence.

  • Su & Henckel (2022) already study query-specific adjustment-coefficient agreement, including the same Sachs sample, graph and 36 queries. A direct RoCA comparison remains unperformed.
  • Padh et al. (2025) bound queries over plausible graphs with prior constraints. No empirical superiority is established.
  • The next scientific gate is a frozen direct comparison and independent decision-relevant evaluation, followed by peer review of this exact revision.
